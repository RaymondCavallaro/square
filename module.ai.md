# **System Summary (Module-Based)**

This module summary describes the target orientation.
The current `square` prototype only implements an earlier reduced slice of it, with declarations acting as early claims and activity history acting as an early memory trail.

## **1. Claim System (Core Unit)**

* Reality is expressed as **claims**
* Each claim defines:

  * intent (what change is sought)
  * context (where it applies)
  * **evaluation surface (ES)** (how it will be judged)
  * commitment (weight + exposure)

> Claims are actionable, bounded proposals of reality.

---

## **2. Graph & Memory System (Structure)**

* All claims form a **connected graph**
* Tracks:

  * dependencies
  * contradictions
  * outcomes
  * ES reuse

> Memory is a structured history of how reality was proposed, tested, and evolved.

---

## **3. Incentive System (Energy & Trust)**

* **Weight** = committed stake tied to outcomes

* **Backing** = contextual support by others

* Incentives:

  * reward correct alignment over time
  * penalize failed or contradictory support

> Legitimacy emerges from aligned commitment under consequence.

---

## **4. Resolution System (Reality Feedback)**

* Claims are executed and evaluated via their ES

* Outcomes:

  * success / failure / partial
  * conflicts expose ambiguity in ES

* Results feed back into:

  * claim validity
  * ES quality

> Truth emerges from outcomes under shared evaluation.

---

## **5. Interface System (Interaction & Scaling)**

* Users interact through **layered complexity**:

  * surface: simple actions (support / act / challenge)
  * guided: explanations and risks
  * deep: full claim and ES design

* Uses:

  * templates
  * inherited structures
  * relevance filtering

> Complexity exists but is progressively compressed for usability.

---

# **Core Mechanism**

> Claims compete and evolve within shared contexts,
> using evaluation surfaces to enable coordination under partial agreement,
> while incentives and outcomes continuously reshape what is trusted, reused, and legitimized.
